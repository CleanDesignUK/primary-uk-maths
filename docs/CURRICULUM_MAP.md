# Matchday Maths: Year 1–6 curriculum map

Updated 4 November 2026. This is a supplementary practice map for England, not a claim to contain every possible question or to cover the separate curricula of all UK nations. It contains 247 named exercise families. Earlier prerequisites can appear in more than one year.

Source: [DfE mathematics programme of study](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study), Crown copyright, Open Government Licence v3.0. Statutory objectives and non-statutory guidance are distinct. Practical work and mathematical discussion remain part of teaching.

## Progression policy

Match-fit evidence requires independent success across question forms and later-day recall. Tables track multiplication, division by the relevant divisor and missing factors separately. Each required table needs all 12 facts with spaced recall and direct/inverse evidence. Other family gates include coverage, accuracy, multiple sessions and later days. A grown-up records practical checks. A 12-shot promotion match then needs at least 90% independent accuracy and the other gates still satisfied. This is an in-game policy, not an official DfE pass mark or a guarantee of permanent memory.
