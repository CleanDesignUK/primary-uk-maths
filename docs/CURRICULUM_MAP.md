# Matchday Maths: curriculum map

Updated 4 November 2026. This map groups England's Year 3 and Year 5 programme of study into digital exercise families. All listed exercise IDs are playable. Earlier prerequisites remain available for both years.

The source is the [Department for Education mathematics programme](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study), issued under the Open Government Licence. Wales uses progression steps, rather than the England year lists. See [Curriculum for Wales](https://hwb.gov.wales/curriculum-for-wales/areas-of-learning-and-experience/mathematics-and-numeracy/descriptions-of-learning/).

This is practice across curriculum objectives, not a claim that a finite question bank contains every possible question. The map identifies practical work that still needs an adult and physical equipment.

## Practical and reasoning work

Digital construction is included for shapes, angles and bar charts. Physical ruler/protractor practice, measuring real quantities, making 3D models, experimenting with capacity and explaining reasoning aloud still need an adult and equipment. Some exercise families sample only part of a wider objective. No score should be treated as proof of the entire objective without discussing the method and varying the representation.

The adaptive route concentrates on the user's requested foundational number facts, crossing 10, individual/mixed tables, and written methods. It adds wider-curriculum samples as pupils progress. The syllabus route offers immediate teacher-directed access to all exercise families, including ones the automatic route has not reached.
