# Matchday Maths: Year 1–6 curriculum audit

Checked on 4 November 2026 against the Department for Education's England mathematics programme. This is an original implementation checklist of mathematical requirements, grouped for product planning, rather than a reproduction of the statutory wording. The programme is Crown copyright and is available under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Statutory wording and non-statutory teaching notes must remain distinct.

## Scope and authority

- The England programme has year-by-year content, with statutory attainment organised by key stage. Schools can sequence topics differently within a key stage. A game year is a practice pathway, not an official school placement or attainment judgement.
- Fluency, reasoning and problem solving cut across every domain. Number-entry drills alone do not demonstrate all three.
- NCETM sequencing is useful for prerequisites, but is not a complete substitute for the statutory map: its prioritisation FAQ excludes Roman numerals and data construction/presentation, and its mastery materials omit the 11 and 12 tables. Include these explicitly.
- No finite app bank contains every possible curriculum question. Appropriate public wording: **“A supplementary learning resource for the basics of England's primary maths curriculum, Years 1–6.”** Avoid a claim of comprehensive assessment or permanent retention.

## Grouped coverage checklist

Each item should map to named exercise families and question variants. **P** means that physical activity or adult observation remains necessary alongside digital practice. **R** means the activity needs a reasoning or contextual variant, rather than only a bare calculation.

### Year 1

- **Y1-N1 Number:** count forwards/backwards across 100, from different starting numbers; read/write numerals to 100; count in 2s, 5s and 10s; one more/less.
- **Y1-N2 Represent:** match objects, pictures, numerals and number lines; compare quantities; numbers 1–20 in words. **P/R**
- **Y1-A1 Calculate:** understand +, − and =; bonds and inverse subtraction facts within 20; addition/subtraction involving one- and two-digit values within 20, including zero.
- **Y1-A2 Apply:** one-step stories, objects/pictures, and a missing number in varied equation positions. **R**
- **Y1-M1 Groups:** supported multiplication/division through equal groups, sharing and arrays. This is not yet a statutory timed-times-table target. **P/R**
- **Y1-F1 Fractions:** identify, make and name equal halves and quarters of shapes, objects and quantities. **P**
- **Y1-ME1 Measure:** compare and begin recording length/height, mass, capacity/volume and time; meaningful measure vocabulary and simple practical problems. **P/R**
- **Y1-ME2 Money:** recognise the values of different coins and notes.
- **Y1-ME3 Time:** event order; days, weeks, months and years; hour/half-hour clock reading and hand drawing. **P**
- **Y1-G1 Shape:** common 2D/3D shapes, including varied orientations/sizes. **P**
- **Y1-P1 Movement:** positional language and whole, half, quarter and three-quarter turns. **P**
- There is **no separate statutory Year 1 statistics strand**. Early sorting/data can be optional enrichment.

### Year 2

- **Y2-N1 Number:** steps of 2, 3 and 5 from zero; steps of 10 from arbitrary starts, both directions; tens/ones; representations, estimates and number lines.
- **Y2-N2 Compare:** order 0–100; <, >, =; numerals and words to at least 100; place-value problems. **R**
- **Y2-A1 Facts:** fluent addition/subtraction within 20; derive related facts to 100.
- **Y2-A2 Calculate:** 2-digit ± ones, ± tens, ± another 2-digit value; three 1-digit addends; concrete, pictorial and mental methods. **P**
- **Y2-A3 Structure:** addition commutativity, subtraction order, inverse checks and missing values; contextual calculation. **R**
- **Y2-M1 Tables:** recall multiplication **and division** for 2, 5 and 10; odd/even numbers.
- **Y2-M2 Structure:** ×, ÷ and = statements; multiplication commutativity versus division order; repeated addition, arrays, sharing/grouping and stories. **P/R**
- **Y2-F1 Fractions:** thirds, quarters, two quarters and three quarters of length, shapes, sets and quantities; simple fraction statements; 2/4 = 1/2. **P**
- **Y2-ME1 Measure:** choose units and tools; m/cm, kg/g, °C, litre/ml; estimate, measure, compare and order. **P**
- **Y2-ME2 Money:** £/p symbols; make totals using different coin combinations; same-unit sums, differences and change. **P/R**
- **Y2-ME3 Time:** sequence/compare intervals; five-minute clock reading and hand drawing, including quarter past/to; 60 minutes/hour and 24 hours/day. **P**
- **Y2-G1 Shape:** 2D sides and vertical symmetry; 3D faces, edges and vertices; 2D surfaces on solids; compare/sort shapes and objects. **P**
- **Y2-P1 Movement:** object/shape patterns; straight movement; clockwise/anticlockwise turns expressed using right angles. **P**
- **Y2-S1 Data:** read **and construct** pictograms, tallies, block diagrams and tables; category counts, sorting, totals and comparisons. **P/R**

### Year 3

- **Y3-N1 Number:** multiples of 4, 8, 50 and 100; 10/100 more or less; three-digit place value; compare/order, represent/estimate and write numbers/words to 1,000. **R**
- **Y3-A1 Mental:** three-digit values ± ones, tens or hundreds.
- **Y3-A2 Written:** column addition/subtraction through three digits; estimation and inverse checking; contextual and missing-number problems. **R**
- **Y3-M1 Tables:** multiplication and division recall for **3, 4 and 8**, while retaining earlier tables.
- **Y3-M2 Apply:** statements from known facts; two-digit × one-digit calculations, moving towards formal methods; missing values, integer scaling and correspondence choices. **R**
- **Y3-F1 Fractions:** count tenths in both directions; tenths from division by 10; unit/non-unit fractions as numbers and set fractions.
- **Y3-F2 Fractions:** visual equivalents with small denominators; same-denominator addition/subtraction within one whole; compare unit fractions and same-denominator fractions; contextual problems. **P/R**
- **Y3-ME1 Measure:** measure, compare, add/subtract m/cm/mm, kg/g, l/ml; perimeter of simple shapes. **P**
- **Y3-ME2 Money:** practical totals/change with pounds and pence. **R**
- **Y3-ME3 Time:** analogue clock including Roman I–XII; 12/24-hour times; nearest minute; seconds/minutes/hours, am/pm/noon/midnight; month lengths and leap years; duration comparisons. **P/R**
- **Y3-G1 Shape:** draw 2D shapes; make, describe and recognise 3D models in different orientations. **P**
- **Y3-G2 Geometry:** angle as turn/shape property; right angles and 2/3/4-right-angle turns; larger/smaller than a right angle; horizontal/vertical and parallel/perpendicular lines.
- **Y3-S1 Data:** read **and present** scaled bars, pictograms and tables; one- and two-step questions. **P/R**

### Year 4

- **Y4-N1 Number:** multiples of 6, 7, 9, 25 and 1,000; 1,000 more/less; count backwards through zero; four-digit place value; order beyond 1,000; representation/estimation.
- **Y4-N2 Number:** round to 10/100/1,000; increasingly large contextual problems; Roman numerals I–C and the significance of zero/place value. **R**
- **Y4-A1 Calculate:** columns through four digits; estimate/inverse-check; two-step contextual calculations and method choice. **R**
- **Y4-M1 Recall:** all multiplication **and division** facts through **12 × 12**, including 11 and 12.
- **Y4-M2 Structure:** zero/one multiplication, division by one, products of three numbers, factor pairs and commutativity.
- **Y4-M3 Written/apply:** 2/3-digit × 1-digit layout; distributive relationships, scaling and harder correspondence contexts. **R**
- **Y4-F1 Fractions:** visual equivalent families; count hundredths; fractional quantities including non-unit fractions with whole-number results; same-denominator addition/subtraction.
- **Y4-F2 Decimals:** tenths/hundredths equivalents; 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 as decimals; divide 1/2-digit numbers by 10/100; identify decimal places.
- **Y4-F3 Decimals:** round 1 d.p. to an integer; compare equal-length decimal values through 2 d.p.; money/measure contexts. **R**
- **Y4-ME1 Measure:** unit conversions; cm/m rectilinear perimeter; area by square counting; estimate/compare/calculate measures and £/p. **P/R**
- **Y4-ME2 Time:** analogue, digital 12/24-hour conversions; hour/minute/second and year/month/week/day problems. **R**
- **Y4-G1 Shape:** classify triangles/quadrilaterals by properties and size; acute/obtuse angles and ordering through 180°.
- **Y4-G2 Symmetry:** lines in different orientations; complete a specified symmetric figure. **P**
- **Y4-P1 Coordinates:** first quadrant; translations by left/right/up/down amounts; plot points and complete polygon sides. **P**
- **Y4-S1 Data:** represent discrete and continuous data, including bars and time graphs; comparison, sum and difference questions. **P/R**

### Year 5

- **Y5-N1 Number:** read/write/order/compare at least 1,000,000; digit values; counting by powers of 10; negative contexts and crossing zero.
- **Y5-N2 Number:** round up to 1,000,000 to 10/100/1,000/10,000/100,000; contextual problems; Roman numerals through M and years. **R**
- **Y5-A1 Calculate:** columns with more than four digits; large-number mental methods; rounded checks/appropriate accuracy; multi-step stories and method choice. **R**
- **Y5-M1 Properties:** multiples, all factor pairs/common factors, prime/composite/prime-factor vocabulary; primality to 100 and remembered primes through 19; squares/cubes and notation.
- **Y5-M2 Written:** values through four digits × 1/2 digits, including long multiplication; short division by one digit with contextual remainders.
- **Y5-M3 Apply:** retained fact recall; whole/decimal ×/÷ 10, 100, 1,000; mixed operations/equality; factors, squares, cubes, fractional scaling and simple rates. **R**
- **Y5-F1 Fractions:** compare denominators with common-multiple relationships; identify/write visual equivalents; improper/mixed conversion and results above one.
- **Y5-F2 Calculate:** add/subtract with equal or related denominators; proper fractions and mixed values × whole numbers, with models. **P**
- **Y5-F3 Decimals:** decimal-as-fraction; thousandths relationships; rounding 2 d.p. to integer/1 d.p.; reading, ordering and problems through 3 d.p. **R**
- **Y5-F4 Percent:** parts per hundred; percentage/fraction/decimal equivalents, especially halves, quarters, fifths and denominators related to 10 or 25. **R**
- **Y5-ME1 Measure:** metric conversions; approximate metric/imperial equivalences; composite rectilinear perimeter.
- **Y5-ME2 Area/volume:** rectangle area in cm²/m²; irregular-area estimates; volume/capacity estimates using cubes or water. **P**
- **Y5-ME3 Apply:** time-unit conversions; all four operations in decimal measure/money/scaling contexts. **R**
- **Y5-G1 Shape:** identify solids from 2D representations; distinguish regular/irregular polygons; deduce rectangle lengths/angles.
- **Y5-G2 Angles:** degrees; acute/obtuse/reflex estimation and comparison; draw/measure given angles; 360° around points, 180° straight lines and multiples of 90°. **P**
- **Y5-P1 Transform:** describe/represent reflection and translation; recognise preserved shape. First-quadrant grids support this. **P**
- **Y5-S1 Data:** line-graph comparison/sum/difference; complete/read tables including timetables. **R**

### Year 6

- **Y6-N1 Number:** numbers through 10,000,000; digit value/order; whole-number rounding to requested accuracy; negative intervals crossing zero and contextual problems. **R**
- **Y6-A1 Written:** long multiplication through 4-digit × 2-digit; 4-digit ÷ 2-digit long division and suitable short-division cases.
- **Y6-A2 Apply:** remainders as whole leftovers, fractions or rounding according to context; large mental/mixed calculations; factors/multiples/primes; operation order; multi-step/all-operation problems; estimated/appropriately rounded checks. **R**
- **Y6-F1 Fractions:** simplify with common factors; common-denominator forms using multiples; compare/order including above one.
- **Y6-F2 Calculate:** unlike-denominator and mixed-number addition/subtraction; proper fraction × proper fraction, simplified; proper fraction ÷ whole number.
- **Y6-F3 Decimals:** fraction-as-division and decimal equivalents; digit values through 3 d.p.; ×/÷ powers of 10; decimal values through 2 d.p. × whole numbers; written division with answers through 2 d.p.; accuracy/rounding problems and fraction/decimal/percentage equivalence. **R**
- **Y6-R1 Ratio:** relative quantities with integer multiplicative relationships; percentage calculations/comparisons; similar-shape scale factors; unequal sharing/grouping. **R**
- **Y6-L1 Algebra:** simple formulae; generate/describe linear sequences; algebraic missing-number statements; pairs satisfying two-unknown equations; enumerate two-variable possibilities. **R**
- **Y6-ME1 Measure:** calculate/convert standard length, mass, volume and time with decimals through 3 d.p.; miles/kilometres; sensible accuracy. **R**
- **Y6-ME2 Area/volume:** same area/different perimeter and vice versa; appropriate formula use; triangle/parallelogram area; compare/estimate/calculate cube/cuboid volume using cm³/m³. **P/R**
- **Y6-G1 Construct:** draw shapes from dimensions/angles; describe/build solids and nets. **P**
- **Y6-G2 Classify/deduce:** shape properties/sizes; missing angles in triangles, quadrilaterals and regular polygons; angles at a point, straight line and vertically opposite angles. **R**
- **Y6-G3 Circles:** name/illustrate radius, diameter and circumference; diameter = twice radius. π-based circumference/area formulae are not required here.
- **Y6-P1 Coordinates:** all four quadrants; draw/translate shapes; reflect in coordinate axes. **P**
- **Y6-S1 Data:** read **and construct** pie charts and line graphs; solve questions; calculate and interpret the mean. **P/R**

## What digital answers cannot establish on their own

These are implementation implications, not extra statutory rules:

1. A virtual ruler, clock, protractor or shape tool can support learning but does not establish correct use of a physical tool. Offer an adult-checked practical list.
2. Include drawing, construction, classification, representation and data presentation. Do not silently substitute “name this shape” for “construct a shape/net” or chart reading for chart creation.
3. Ask pupils to explain or demonstrate a strategy to a grown-up. Written answer speed combines knowledge, reading and input skills, and cannot by itself prove conceptual understanding.
4. Distinguish **digital coverage**, **independent recall**, **spaced retention**, **reasoning/application**, and **adult-observed practical work** in reports. A curriculum mapping is an editorial link, not an externally validated attainment claim.
5. Any year-promotion threshold is the app's transparent practice policy. Show the missing objectives and evidence; avoid claiming DfE-approved “pass”, a school promotion, or mastery from XP alone. Prior learning and due review should survive promotion.

## Times-table implementation priorities

| Stage | Statutory focus | Product implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Year 1 | Supported equal groups, arrays, sharing; counting 2/5/10 | Build meaning with pictures before short recall drills. |
| Year 2 | 2, 5, 10 with inverse division; odd/even | Track multiplication and division evidence separately. |
| Year 3 | 3, 4, 8, retaining earlier facts | Individual recall, connected doubling, mixed retrieval, inverse facts, missing factors, application. |
| Year 4 | All facts to 12 × 12 and related division | Include 11/12, mixed tables, zero/one structure, products of three values and factor pairs. |
| Years 5–6 | Retained fluency used in richer calculations | Continue due review while applying facts in written methods, fractions, ratio and measure. |

Recommended app rules, not official assessment rules: keep per-fact evidence, not only a table-wide accuracy average; require independent success across different days; keep early “ready” distinct from long-term recall; cap new facts per session; return mistakes after intervening questions; mix familiar tables with the current focus; include both multiplication orientations but do not let commutativity manufacture independent division evidence. Do not mark “all tables complete” from one easy mixed run.

If an optional MTC-style practice mode is marketed, the official format is 25 multiplication questions after 3 practice questions, with 6 seconds per answer. The official check has no pass mark. It does not assess division, application or strategies. An app may offer other timings for learning/accessibility, but should label those as its own training modes rather than the official format.
